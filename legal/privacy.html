Privacy Policy

Crypto Network Match Bot (“we”, “the Service”)
Telegram community: Crypto Network Community
How we handle personal data for the professional crypto matching network
Last updated: August 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how personal data is processed when you use the Crypto Network Match Bot on Telegram, the Crypto Network Community group, related community features, and optional HTTP admin/API operations run by the service operator.

Telegram user IDs and usernames are personal data. Telegram’s own privacy policy also applies to data Telegram processes independently of us.

2. Lawful basis (summary)

We process data to:

  • Perform the contract you request: onboarding, matching, notifications, verification steps you start;
  • Legitimate interests: abuse prevention, safety rails, fraud review (e.g. OTC match flags), service reliability;
  • Consent: optional fields, community-share toggles, optional work-email verification;
  • Legal obligation where applicable (e.g. responding to lawful requests).

3. Data we collect

  • Telegram identity: numeric Telegram user ID, @username (if set), language code provided by Telegram, and group membership signals for the configured community chat
  • Profile & matching: display name, affiliation, bio, primary/secondary roles, intents, chains, language/region, skills/tags, lifecycle or budget bands you enter, capacity prefs, matching opt-in/out, privacy toggles
  • Verification: level (L0–L4), voluntary L1 self-reported social proofs, L2 work-email challenge state (and email address while a challenge is outstanding), reviewer notes limited to verification workflow
  • Network activity: match proposals and outcomes, accept/decline timestamps, ratings, reputation counters, check-in responses, referral codes, blocks, and reports you submit or that name you
  • Technical: application logs and security events needed to operate, rate-limit, and debug the Service

We do not ask for seed phrases, private keys, payment card numbers, or wallet secrets. Do not send them.

4. How we use data

  • Run onboarding and profile management
  • Score and propose professional introductions within product rules
  • Enforce double opt-in reveal before sharing contact details
  • Send service messages (matches, reminders, intent refresh, safety notices)
  • Operate verification, shortlists, and restricted capital/OTC rails
  • Moderate the community, investigate reports, suspend or ban for abuse
  • Improve reliability and prevent fraud or farming

We do not sell personal data. We do not use the Service to distribute trading signals. We do not take custody of your assets.

5. Sharing

Before mutual accept: counterparties see a limited match card (roles, verification badge labels, chains, intent, language/region, and other fields shown as the product defines), with self-reported claims labelled where applicable. Your Telegram username is not shown until both sides opt in.

After mutual accept: we may share @username and the fuller intro card needed to continue a professional conversation.

Community announcements: if you join the group and have not disabled relevant privacy options, we may post a limited welcome or milestone line (e.g. display name, role). You control share toggles in Settings where offered.

Processors: infrastructure providers (e.g. cloud host, MongoDB host, optional email delivery for L2) process data under contract solely to run the Service. Admins may access reports, verifications, and flagged OTC/capital matches for safety.

6. Retention

  • Active accounts: while you use the Service
  • After deletion request: we erase or anonymize your profile and personal identifiers as described below, and keep only what is needed for security, dispute handling, or legal obligation for a reasonable period
  • Match/rating history: may be retained in anonymized or de-identified form for abuse analytics and product measurement
  • Email challenges: OTP and pending email state are short-lived and discarded after verification succeeds, expires, or is cancelled

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access / export: use /export or Settings → Export to receive a copy of your stored profile-related data
  • Erasure: use /delete or Settings → Delete account for erasure with match/rating anonymization as implemented
  • Correction: update profile fields via Settings and onboarding edit flows
  • Matching opt-out: disable matching in Settings without full deletion
  • Community share: privacy toggles where available

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may also have rights to object, restrict processing, or lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us to exercise rights we cannot fulfil solely through bot commands.

8. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures (API authentication for admin routes, access controls, logging redaction of secret headers). No electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Impersonation risk on Telegram remains high—verify counterparties independently.

9. International users

Data may be processed and stored where our operators and subprocessors run (for example MongoDB Atlas and cloud hosting regions). By using the Bot you understand such transfers may occur.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy and will revise the date above. Continued use after an update constitutes acknowledgement of the revised Policy.

12. Contact

Privacy requests: message Crypto Network Match Bot (@founder_collab_bot) or administrators of Crypto Network Community.

This Policy aligns with Crypto Network Match Bot product rules (introduction-only network, verification labels, export/delete, safety). Have qualified counsel review controller identity, DPAs with your hosts, and local law before large-scale launch.
